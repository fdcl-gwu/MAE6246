One measurement, one update

x ∼ N(x̄, M),   z = x + v,   v ∼ N(0, R)

The prior position and measurement noise are independent. After observing z, the posterior is x | z ∼ N(x̂, P). Its mean x̂ is the MMSE estimate; P measures the uncertainty that remains.

Reading the measurement curve

The dashed curve is the likelihood p(z | x), viewed as a function of candidate position x. For this sensor model it has the shape N(z, R) and integrates to one over x. It shows which positions agree with the observed z. The posterior is proportional to prior × likelihood.
